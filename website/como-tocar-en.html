The screen

In the centre, the sphere of water with the five organisms; around it, the cards, numbered 01 to 11; below, the strip of desire; above, the header.

The first control of each card is its door: that module's amount in the mix. At zero the module is off and the other controls are greyed out. Raise the door first, then move the others. Everything is slow on purpose: give each gesture a minute.

ECOSYSTEM

The aquarium, the web and the music

Five organisms live in a sphere of water: they see, hear, hunt, flee, court and defend territory. The coloured lines are the web of relations; the panel below shows the state of the water, the tension, the key and the latest events. Press and hold on the sphere to move the lens and listen closer.

Try: Watch a coral line (a hunt) grow thick, then listen for the terminal buzz and the capture; after it, a cadence.

The controls
COHESIONHow tightly the school swims and sings together.
DANGERFear in the water: sharper accents, more ornaments, more flights.
SPARSE FOCUSListening mode: few, focused events.
ENERGY MEMORYListening mode: energy remembered and released slowly.
GEOMETRIC CYCLEListening mode: the orbits' cycles lead.
COLLECTIVE DRIFTListening mode: the group drifts together.

ECOLOGY

Desire and geometry

The lower strip holds the desire of the ecosystem (density, drift, morph) and the geometric sequencer of the orbits. Desire changes tendencies, never writes events.

Try: ECO CONTROLS (in the aquarium) opens cohesion, danger and the listening modes, and the scenes.

The controls
DENSITYHow much happens: the ecosystem's desire for density.
DRIFTAgitation: left is calm water, right is restless.
NEUTRAL ← ECOSYSTEM MORPH → ACTIVEFrom a neutral mix to the ecosystem's full spatial and timbral influence.
GEOMETRY PERFORMANCEZero: autonomous geometry. Raise to enable rhythm, rotation, intervals and mutation; changes appear at the next events.
RHYTHM DENSITYHow many steps of each orbit sound.
ORBIT ROTATIONRotates where each orbit's pattern begins.
INTERVAL BIASLeans the melodic steps towards smaller or wider intervals.
MUTATIONHow often the orbits change their shape.
AUTONOMOUS BIOMEThe biome lives and sounds by itself; turn off to play it only from outside.
EXTERNAL ENERGY GATEExternal notes (MIDI, keyboard) gate the biome's energy.
VARYVARY: the controls move by themselves over the music's time, slowly and in relation, around where the seed or your hand left them - warmer and quicker at the climax of the piece, settling at rest. The output stage never moves. Move any control and it breathes around your value. Off: everything stays where it is. Each opening also proposes another biome - water, light, population, bodies, which modules wake; the state you left is kept as opening.previousStateV1 in the settings.

BRAIN

Uncertainty and state

The bio-energy and the internal decisions of the biome: it needs no external note to live. Its state feeds every other card.

Try: Leave the biome alone for a few minutes and listen to how it moves through day and season.

The controls
CLOCKBRAIN: how often the Brain's register turns (0.06 to 0.9 Hz). Centre = as before.
COORDINATIONBRAIN: how much the organisms share one leaning - at 0 each its own, at 1 a school. Zero = as before.

GEOMETRY

Form, structure and the ecological music

Three layers of composition: FORM (a Markov memory of sections), STRUCTURE (step memory and harmony) and the MUSIC of the ecosystem, where events are born from the web of relations between the organisms and from the state of the water.

Try: Raise MUSIC to 1, then move WATER slowly to the left: listen to the music freeze into a crystal; then to the right, into turbulence and boiling.

The controls
FORM MIXZero: formal memory transparent. Raise to move section and contour at the next collective events.
CONTEXTHow much the ecological state attracts the next formal region.
INERTIATendency to stay in the current formal region.
MUTATIONDistributed probability of jumps between formal regions.
STRUCTURE MIXZero: previous path intact. Raise to enable step memory and progression.
STEP MEMORYContribution of the addressable memory to pitch and duration of events.
HARMONYContribution of the weighted chords to the pitch field.
PHASE PATHEcological path: day on the left, season on the right.
MUSICZero: previous route intact. Raise so that the music comes from the ecosystem: hunt, flight, courtship, territory, the web of relations.
REACTIONHow strongly the organisms react to what they see and hear through the water.
MEMORYLeft: songs repeat (identity). Right: they transform and are copied among allies.
WATERCentre: the water follows the season. Left cools towards ice; right heats towards boiling. The change of state takes the time of latent heat.

TIMBRE

The organisms' bodies

Each organism can sing with a second body - voice, matter, breath, string, drop, fold, membrane, glass, choir, bow or reed - mixed with its native body. ECOLOGICAL TIMBRE lets the water, the hunt, the flight, the courtship, vigour and health transform them. Below, SYNTHESIS and EDIT open the synthesis device: a voice per organism that you build, played by the ecosystem.

Try: Give MORPH the voice and PULSAR the drop, raise their MIX, then ECOLOGICAL TIMBRE: when the hunter chases, its voice sharpens; when the water boils, the drops thicken.

The controls
BODYThis organism's second body: voice (formant song), matter (harmonic → membrane modes), breath, string, water drop, fold, membrane (water drum → skin), rubbed glass, choir, bow (a bowed string) or reed (a clarinet's tube).
MATTERMATTER: the character inside the body (the vowels of voice and choir; harmonic → membrane; the air of breath; plucked → bowed; drops → rain; the fold; loose drum → taut skin; the brightness and beating of glass; the bow from sul tasto to sul ponticello; the reed's embouchure, dark to bright).
MIXMIX: how much of the second body sounds together with the native body.
ECOLOGICAL TIMBREECOLOGICAL TIMBRE: how much the ecosystem's life transforms the matter and presence of the bodies (water, hunt, flight, courtship, vigour, health).
SUPPORTSUPPORT: the level of the three support organisms - GRAIN, MODAL, STRING - against MORPH and PULSAR, from -6 to +24 dB. They sit about 22-26 dB under the foreground; raise it to bring them forward. 0 dB at the start.

The synthesis device

A voice you build, for each organism

TIMBRE → EDIT opens a synthesiser per organism: two sources (oscillator, noise, FM, wavetable, additive, pulsar, string, matter, chaos, feedback or one of the bodies), RING and FM between them, a filter, two envelopes and two LFOs. The ecosystem plays the notes; in MODULATION, the biome's life - hunger, tension, distance, the water's temperature - moves the parameters you route.

Try: PROPOSE ALL, and SYNTHESIS at half; then change one route at a time and listen to what the ecosystem does with it. The tutorial has five lessons on the device.

The controls
PROPOSEPROPOSE: a starting patch for this organism, drawn from its species (sources, gesture, register) and its ecological strategy (what reaches the matrix), and levelled with the others. Each press draws another one; the first of a session follows its seed. Edit from there.
MIXSYNTHESIS: how much of each organism's synthesis voice sounds instead of its own body. At zero the instrument sounds exactly as before.

SYNTHESIS

Organic voices

The granular Striker: impacts made of particles, from dust to resonant metal.

Try: Raise STRIKER MIX, then move MATTER and SCATTER while the biome plays.

The controls
STRIKER MIXZero: autonomous Striker. Raise to enable MATTER, POP and SCATTER; they answer at the next impacts.
MATTERFrom short dust to resonant metal.
POPNumber of particles per impact.
SCATTERTemporal and spectral scatter.

SPACE

Perspective and memory

The shared space where the organisms' sounds reflect: early reflections whose size, opening and tone follow the aquarium.

Try: Raise SPACE MIX and SIZE for a large hall of water; lower TONE for a deeper, darker space.

The controls
RETURNAudible amount of the shared space.
SPACE MIXZero: autonomous Space. Raise to enable SIZE, SPREAD, TONE and MEMORY. RETURN works independently.
SIZETime scale of the reflections.
SPREADLateral opening of the reflections.
TONESpectral opening of the reflections.
MEMORYPersistence of the reflection field.

ENVIRONMENT

Signature and bed

An eight-band spectral signature of the biome and a bed of noise guided by season, water and the glass's integrity.

Try: Raise ENV MIX and AMBIENCE a little: the aquarium gains its own air.

The controls
ENV MIXZero: transparent bypass. Raise to enable filter and atmosphere.
FILTERDry mix of the eight-band spectral signature.
CENTRELogarithmic centre of the spectral bank.
SPANSpread in octaves of the eight bands.
RESONANCESelectivity of the spectral bands.
AMBIENCENoise bed guided by season, water and integrity.

TAIL

Synthetic persistence

A late, diffuse reverberation after the first reflections.

Try: With NIGHTMARE the tail twists on itself: use it sparingly, as an event.

The controls
TAIL MIXZero: transparent bypass. Raise to hear the late tail.
DECAYLength of the synthetic persistence.
DAMPINGProgressive absorption of the high frequencies.
NIGHTMARETwist of the feedback matrix.

MASTER

Output and integrity

The final level, the safety limiter and an optional creative master (multiband dynamics, width, glue).

Try: OUTPUT TRIM is the volume. MUTE and PAUSE are in the header.

The controls
CREATIVE MASTERMacro of crossover, multiband dynamics, width and optical glue. Zero is an exact bypass.
OUTPUT TRIMFinal technical level: -18 dB to +6 dB; 0.75 is 0 dB.
CEILINGLimiter ceiling: -6 dB to -0.1 dB; initially -1 dB.
RELEASELimiter recovery: 50 ms to 500 ms; initially 150 ms.
EQ ORDERWhere the CREATIVE MASTER's tilt EQ sits: BEFORE the multiband dynamics (the dynamics react to the tilted sound) or AFTER them (the tilt colours what the dynamics left). It acts only with CREATIVE MASTER above zero.
MULTIBANDCREATIVE MASTER: the multiband dynamics, less or more than the ecology sets. Centre = the ecology alone.
GLUECREATIVE MASTER: the slow glue compressor, less or more. Centre = the ecology alone.
TILTCREATIVE MASTER: darker (left) or brighter (right) than the daylight sets. Centre = the ecology alone.
WIDTHCREATIVE MASTER: narrower or wider than the cohesion sets (the bass stays mono). Centre = the ecology alone.

Lens

The lens on the glass

Press and hold on the sphere to put the lens against the glass and listen closely to the creatures passing there; the rest goes out of focus. The four knobs give the lens its shape.

Try: Follow an organism with the lens as it crosses the sphere.

The controls
RADIUSLENS: the size of the focused spot on the glass. Centre = as before.
REACHLENS: how deep into the water the lens reaches from the glass. Centre = as before.
ZOOMLENS: how much the focused organisms come forward. Centre = as before.
BLURLENS: how far the organisms out of focus fall back. Centre = as before.

Meters

The output, measured

Loudness (momentary, short-term and integrated LUFS), the true peak, the safety limiter's reduction, the stereo correlation and the three taps the ecosystem can listen to: before the CREATIVE MASTER, before the limiter and after it.

The header

Record, mute, listen and learn

The output volume, REC, MUTE and PAUSE, SPEAKERS or HEADPHONES, the AUDIO device, LEARN, the language, TUTORIAL and ABOUT. The status line shows the session's world and its seed.

Try: Turn LEARN on and hover over any control.

The controls
RECRecords what you hear (after MASTER and MUTE) to a 32-bit float WAV in Music/Aquorbium/<date>/, with a text file beside it: the session seed and the controls. Up to one hour.
MUTECloses the sound with a short ramp; the ecosystem keeps living.
PAUSESilences and freezes the ecosystem's time; RESUME continues where it stopped.
SPEAKERS / HEADPHONESSPEAKERS: the stereo mix. HEADPHONES: each organism heard from where it is in the sphere - left and right, front and back, above and below - through a head (Brown & Duda's binaural model: the head's shadow, the delay between the ears, the pinna). Use it with headphones only.
AUDIOChoose output, rate and buffer. To diagnose clicks, try 48 kHz / 512 samples.
LEARNLEARN on: point at any control or card and its explanation appears below
